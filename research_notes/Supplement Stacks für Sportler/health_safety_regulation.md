## 1. Micronutrients and basics: who needs what (only when tested or deficient)

### Takeaway
For a healthy, omnivorous German hypertrophy trainee, only low-dose vitamin D (≤20 µg = 800 IU/day, mainly in the dark season) is defensible without testing. Vegans must take B12 permanently. Iodine should come mainly from iodized salt, and iodine supplements (≤100 µg) are not for people with thyroid disease. Iron, zinc, magnesium, K2, multivitamins and extra sodium make sense only with a proven deficiency or a specific situation. Omega-3 is optional if fish is eaten 1–2×/week. Doses of ≥1 g/day EPA+DHA carry a dose-dependent atrial-fibrillation (AF) signal in people with cardiovascular (CV) disease or CV risk factors.

#### Vitamin D
- DGE reference value: **20 µg/day, applicable only when the body makes no vitamin D itself**. It was set to reach a 25(OH)D serum level of **50 nmol/L**. Normal diets supply 2–4 µg/day in adolescents and adults, and the gap must be closed by skin synthesis and/or a supplement. The recommendation applies from age 1 year when sun exposure is insufficient — [DGE press release "Neue Referenzwerte für Vitamin D"](https://www.dge.de/presse/meldungen/pressearchiv/standard-titel-1/) (professional society; reference value from 2012 — year per [PZ 06/2012](https://www.pharmazeutische-zeitung.de/ausgabe-062012/das-hormon-der-streithaehne/)); [DGE reference values page](https://www.dge.de/wissenschaft/referenzwerte/vitamin-d/)
- DGE: good vitamin D supply in **older people** lowers the risk of falls, fractures, strength loss and early death — same DGE sources (snippet)
- German status (RKI, DEGS1 data, published 2016): **30.2%** of adults <30 nmol/L (deficient), **61.6%** <50 nmol/L, and only 38.4% adequate. In **winter 52%** of adults were <30 nmol/L versus **8.3% in summer**. Thresholds: <30 nmol/L deficient, 30–50 suboptimal, ≥50 adequate — [RKI Journal of Health Monitoring 2/2016](https://www.rki.de/DE/Aktuelles/Publikationen/Journal-of-Health-Monitoring/GBEDownloadsJ/FactSheets/JoHM_2016_02_ernaehrung4.html) (federal public-health institute; **old data**, survey fieldwork 2008–2011 per background knowledge); [DEGS1 paper](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4499202/)
- BfR worst case: if all fortified foods carried the proposed maxima (including UV-treated milk and mushrooms), adult intake at the 95th percentile would be about 37 µg/day, so 20 µg per supplement dose is considered safe — [BfR 2023 update](https://www.bfr.bund.de/cm/343/aktualisierung-2023-hoechstmengenvorschlaege-fuer-vitamin-d-in-lebensmitteln-inklusive-nahrungsergaenzungsmitteln.pdf)
- BfR 065/2023 (7 Dec 2023): taken long term alongside other sources, products with **≥4000 IU per daily dose** can lead to a health-concerning total intake. Vitamin D poisoning has been reported in children and adults. Overdose shows as **hypercalcaemia**, with fatigue, muscle weakness, nausea, **cardiac arrhythmias** and weight loss — [BfR Stellungnahme 065/2023](https://www.bfr.bund.de/cm/343/hochdosierte-nahrungsergaenzungsmittel-mit-vitamin-d-koennen-langfristig-die-gesundheit-beeintraechtigen.pdf); related: [BfR 055/2023 "Vitamin D: sinnvoll oder überflüssig?", 31 Oct 2023](https://www.bfr.bund.de/cm/343/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel-mit-vitamin-d-sinnvoll-oder-ueberfluessig.pdf) [title only]
- **BfR 31/2025 (3 Sep 2025)** on bolus products (high single doses every few days or weeks):
  - Very high single doses (up to 500 µg every 20 days) produced problematic blood levels in people who were already well supplied, and higher risks of **falls and fractures** were observed.
  - 10- and 20-day schedules are hard to follow, so a second dose may be taken too early.
  - Recommendation: if needed, take a **daily low dose of ≤20 µg**. Bolus doses, especially combined with vitamin K, should be used **only on medical instruction and under supervision**.
  - Sources: [BfR Stellungnahme 31/2025](https://www.bfr.bund.de/assets/01_Ver%C3%B6ffentlichungen/Stellungnahmen_deutsch/einnahme-hoher-einzeldosen-vitamin-d-ueber-nahrungsergaenzungsmittel-im-abstand-von-tagen-oder-wochen-birgt-gesundheitliche-risiken.pdf); [PTAheute, 18 Sep 2025](https://www.ptaheute.de/aktuelles/2025/09/18/bfr-vitamin-d-besser-taeglich-und-niedrig-dosiert-einnehmen) (pharmacy trade press); [Gelbe Liste](https://www.gelbe-liste.de/apotheke/vitamin-d-risiken)
- Endocrine Society guideline (published 3 Jun 2024 in JCEM):
  - It suggests **against routine 25(OH)D testing** in all populations considered (including obesity and dark skin), and against routine follow-up tests to guide the dose.
  - Healthy adults under 75 should take the RDA. The guideline aligns with USPSTF and Choosing Wisely.
  - Sources: [Endocrine Society newsroom](https://www.endocrine.org/news-and-advocacy/news-room/2024/endocrine-society-recommends-healthy-adults-take-the-recommended-daily-allowance-of-vitamin-d) (medical society, Jun 2024); [Medscape](https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/dont-screen-vitamin-d-new-endo-society-guideline-2024a1000aez); [PubMed 38828931](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38828931/)
- Stiftung Warentest, issue 12/2025:
  - 25 vitamin D supplements were tested: **6 rated "gut", 2 failed**, and strongly overdosed products failed. Many cheap products did well, and price said little about quality — [test.de](https://www.test.de/Vitamin-D-Praeparate-im-Test-6257752-0/) via the secondary summary [lomazoma](https://lomazoma.com/stiftung-warentest-zu-vitamin-d-nur-6-von-25-produkten-gut/) [weak].
  - [conflict] Another summary says only **2** products were judged suitable without restriction — [bundle.app](https://www.bundle.app/de/nachrichten/vitamin-d-zwei-praparate-halt-stiftung-warentest-fur-geeignet-16D11BF6-D0F2-45FF-AE6C-E3A874DE60FF) [weak]. The difference may be quality grade versus suitability for long-term use at ≤20 µg.
  - Stiftung Warentest considers vitamin D products superfluous for many people and most products too high-dosed — [Deutsches Ärzteblatt](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/news/stiftung-warentest-vitamin-d-praparate-fur-viele-menschen-uberflussig-42117848-8bb2-4877-b2de-f2cc33ed97db) (medical press, ~Dec 2025).

#### Omega-3 (EPA/DHA)
- EFSA: **250 mg EPA+DHA/day** is the adequate intake for adults and is enough to maintain normal cardiac function — [EFSA DRV summary tables](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/assets/DRV_Summary_tables_jan_17.pdf) (EU agency; DRV from 2010, table version 2017)
- EFSA 2012: supplemental EPA+DHA up to 5 g/day did not increase bleeding episodes, impair glucose regulation or impair immune function in adults. EPA alone up to 1.8 g/day raises no concern — [EFSA Journal 2012;10(7):2815](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.2903/j.efsa.2012.2815)
- **EFSA 2026** (adopted 15 Dec 2025, published Jan 2026): keeps the safe level of **1 g/day supplemental DHA alone** for all groups, including children and pregnant/lactating women. It applies to DHA-only or mostly-DHA sources (EPA/DHA ratio <0.3: fish-oil concentrates, algal oils, krill oils) in any chemical form — [EFSA Journal 2026;9858](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2026.9858)
- **EMA PRAC (autumn 2023)**:
  - Meta-analyses of RCTs with **>80,000 patients**, mostly with CV disease or CV risk factors, showed a **dose-dependent AF risk** with omega-3-acid ethyl-ester medicines versus placebo.
  - About **1 g/day → about +12%**; **1.8–4 g/day → about +50%**; the risk was highest at 4 g/day.
  - AF is now listed as a "common" adverse reaction. Patients should seek care if they have palpitations or shortness of breath.
  - Sources: [EMA DHPC](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/dhpc/direct-healthcare-professional-communication-dhpc-omega-3-acid-ethyl-ester-medicines-dose-dependent-increased-risk-atrial-fibrillation-patients-established-cardiovascular-diseases-or-cardiovascular_en.pdf) (EU regulator, 2023); [BfArM Rote-Hand-Brief, 16 Nov 2023](https://www.bfarm.de/SharedDocs/Risikoinformationen/Pharmakovigilanz/DE/RHB/2023/rhb-omega-3-fettsaeure.html) (German regulator); [AIFA note, 8 Nov 2023](https://www.aifa.gov.it/documents/20142/1804929/2023.11.08_NII_omega-3_EN.pdf)
- **BfR**: omega-3 preparations can raise AF risk in people with **existing or impending heart disease**. The underlying studies used both medicines and **food supplements**, and supplements can be taken long term without medical supervision. Eating fish 1–2×/week remains health-promoting — [BfR communication](https://www.bfr.bund.de/veroeffentlichung/praeparate-mit-omega-3-fettsaeuren-koennen-bei-herzpatienten-das-risiko-fuer-vorhofflimmern-erhoehen/) [date?; after the 2023 EMA review]; [ZHAW repost](https://www.zhaw.ch/de/lsfm/institute-zentren/ilgi/qm-und-lebensmittelrecht/news/detailansicht-news/event-news/bfr-praeparate-mit-omega-3-fettsaeuren-koennen-bei-herzpatienten-das-risiko-fuer-vorhofflimmern-erhoehen)
- Stiftung Warentest (older test [date?]):
  - 20 omega-3 supplements (14 fish oil, 4 algae oil, 2 linseed oil), costing **5–90 cents per day**.
  - It judged them unnecessary because a benefit for CV protection is not sufficiently proven.
  - Sources: [Ärzteblatt](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/news/stiftung-warentest-haelt-mittel-mit-omega-3-fettsaeuren-fuer-ueberfluessig-d8578977-2b34-47fc-8ab7-4d440c221b55); [test.de](https://www.test.de/Pillen-fuer-die-Schule-Leere-Versprechen-4494129-0/)

#### Magnesium
- The SCF UL of 250 mg/day for supplemental Mg rests on mild diarrhoea in a small share of adults at about **360–365 mg/day**. It covers readily soluble salts (chloride, sulphate, aspartate, lactate) in supplements, fortified foods and mineral water. Magnesium from normal food is excluded — [BfR press release](https://www.bfr.bund.de/en/press-release/bfr-assesses-maximum-daily-level-for-magnesium-in-food-supplements/); [FH Münster](https://www.fh-muenster.de/en/ernaehrung-therapie/news-aus-dem-nut/bioverfuegbarkeit-magnesium) (university)
- A 2023 perspective in *Advances in Nutrition* calls for re-evaluating the magnesium UL for adults — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2161831323013352) [title only; direction of argument not verified]
- Öko-Test (reported as a 2026 test [date?]):
  - 28 magnesium products; **every second one exceeds the BfR 250 mg**. Doppelherz and Tetesept products contain 400 mg per maximum daily dose.
  - Some contain critical additives described as "potentially carcinogenic" (talc is named in a headline).
  - 300 mg/day can cause diarrhoea in some adults, and magnesium supplements are generally **unnecessary for healthy adults**.
  - Sources: [Öko-Test](https://www.oekotest.de/gesundheit-medikamente/magnesiumpraeparate-im-test-viele-sind-ueberdosiert-und-haben-kritische-zusatzstoffe_601194_1.html) (consumer test magazine); [ZDFheute](https://www.zdfheute.de/ratgeber/oeko-test-magnesium-praeparate-unnoetig-100.html); [joyn newstime](https://newstime.joyn.de/themen/oeko-test/wahrscheinlich-krebserregend-oeko-test-warnt-vor-talkum-in-magnesium-praeparaten-130738); [Pflüger blog, "Öko-Test 2026"](https://www.pflueger.de/magazin/magnesium-ueberdosierung-oekotest-2026/) [weak; manufacturer blog]

#### Zinc and copper
- BfR: at most 6.5 mg/day of zinc in food supplements, with a label note above 3.5 mg. Fortifying everyday foods with zinc is not recommended — [BfR zinc proposal](https://www.bfr.bund.de/cm/343/hoechstmengenvorschlaege-fuer-zink-in-lebensmitteln-inklusive-nahrungsergaenzungsmitteln.pdf)
- Too much zinc disturbs **copper metabolism**. Chronically high zinc intake can cause anaemia, neuromotor disorders and a weakened immune system — [Verbraucherzentrale "Mehr Zink bei Erkältungen?"](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/lebensmittel/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel/mehr-zink-bei-erkaeltungen-13390) (consumer organization) [date?]
- The BVL/BfArM Joint Expert Commission counts zinc exposure from all sources. If the SCF UL of 25 mg/day is exceeded, the product's legal status must be examined (food versus medicine) — [BfArM statement on zinc, status 9 May 2022](https://www.bfarm.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/Arzneimittel/Zulassung/ZulRelThemen/abgrenzung/Expertenkommission/STN_Zink.pdf?__blob=publicationFile); [BVL notice, 3 Jul 2023](https://www.bvl.bund.de/SharedDocs/Fachmeldungen/01_lebensmittel/2023/2023_07_03_Stellungnahme_Zink.html) (exact consequence not in the snippet)

#### Iron (only with proven deficiency)
- Athletes over 15: ferritin **<15 µg/L = deficiency**; **15–30 µg/L = low stores** — [SEMS journal, "Iron deficiency in athletes – new guidelines"](https://sems-journal.ch/1839) (Swiss sports medicine society) [date?]
- Latent iron deficiency in athletes is defined from ferritin **<30 µg/L**, even without anaemia — [IMD Berlin lab info](https://www.imd-berlin.de/fachinformationen/diagnostikinformationen/400-499/400-ferritin-bei-eisenmangel-und-eisenmangelanaemie-neue-entscheidungsgrenzen-ermoeglichen-eine-differenziertere-beurteilung) (medical lab); [compeat.ch, Jan 2024](https://compeat.ch/2024/01/12/eisenmangel-im-sport-update/)
- In athletes with ferritin **>70 µg/L**, supplementation has no effect on performance — one of [sportsandmedicine.com, 2022](https://sportsandmedicine.com/de/2022/01/eisenmangel-im-sport/) or [compeat.ch 2024](https://compeat.ch/2024/01/12/eisenmangel-im-sport-update/) [the snippet did not say which]
- Ferritin alone is not reliable for diagnosing clinically relevant iron deficiency in athletes. Measure **haemoglobin, ferritin, transferrin saturation and CRP** (to exclude inflammation) — [German Journal of Sports Medicine 2010](https://www.germanjournalsportsmedicine.com/archive/archive-2010/heft-6/die-hypoferritinaemie-des-sportlers-ist-kein-sicheres-indiz-fuer-eisenmangel/); [IMD Berlin](https://www.imd-berlin.de/fachinformationen/diagnostikinformationen/400-499/400-ferritin-bei-eisenmangel-und-eisenmangelanaemie-neue-entscheidungsgrenzen-ermoeglichen-eine-differenziertere-beurteilung)
- A "target of about 100 ng/mL" circulates in endurance-athlete blogs — [pushing-limits](https://pushing-limits.de/blog/pushinglimits-blog/eisen-warum-es-fuer-triathleten-wichtig-ist/), [trailrunning24, Aug 2026](https://www.trailrunning24.de/2026/08/17/ferritinwert-sportler/) [weak; not a guideline value]
- EFSA 2024: the safe level of **40 mg/day** (adults, including pregnancy and lactation) comes from trials in which black stools did not occur at 20–25 mg/day of supplemental iron plus 15 mg/day from food. Children range from 10 mg/day (1–3 y) to 35 mg/day (15–17 y) — [EFSA Journal 2024;8819](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2024.8819); [NutraIngredients, 13 Jun 2024](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2024/06/13/EFSA-establishes-new-safe-level-for-iron-intake/) (trade press)

#### Vitamin B12 (vegans and vegetarians)
- DGE position paper 2024:
  - A vegan diet can be health-promoting for healthy adults **only with a permanent B12 supplement**, a well-planned diet and possibly other supplements. Iodine needs particular attention.
  - Critical nutrients: B12, iodine, protein, long-chain omega-3, vitamin D, B2, calcium, iron, zinc and selenium.
  - It is not recommended for pregnant or breastfeeding women, infants, children or adolescents.
  - Sources: [DGE press release 2024](https://www.dge.de/presse/meldungen/2024/positionspapier-zu-veganer-ernaehrung/); [Ernährungs Umschau 7/2024, long version](https://ernaehrungs-umschau.de/fileadmin/Ernaehrungs-Umschau/pdfs/pdf_2024/07_24/DGE_Position_Vegan_2024_Langversion.pdf); [Ärzteblatt](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/news/was-die-deutsche-gesellschaft-fuer-ernaehrung-von-veganer-ernaehrung-haelt-64e62d6d-a87d-4416-a54d-cd820ccf19c2)

#### Iodine (supply situation in Germany)
- Iodine supply has been declining since the early 2000s. **32% of adults** and **44% of children and adolescents** are at higher risk of low intake. #### Vitamin K2
- Vitamin K1 and K2 can **weaken coumarin-type anticoagulants**. People on anticoagulants should consult a doctor before taking vitamin K supplements. Whether combining D with K2 has health benefits is unclear, and there are too few data for a risk assessment — [BfR vitamin K proposal](https://www.bfr.bund.de/cm/343/hoechstmengenvorschlaege-fuer-vitamin-k-in-lebensmitteln-inklusive-nahrungsergaenzungsmitteln.pdf); [Verbraucherzentrale "Vitamin-K-Produkte"](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/lebensmittel/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel/vitaminkprodukte-was-ist-sinnvoll-26574); [PTAheute, 9 Jan 2024 "BfR: Wechselwirkung zwischen Vitamin D und K"](https://www.ptaheute.de/aktuelles/2024/01/09/bfr-wechselwirkung-zwischen-vitamin-d-und-k) [title only]
- High-dose (bolus) D+K2 combination products should be taken only under medical supervision — [BfR 31/2025](https://www.bfr.bund.de/assets/01_Ver%C3%B6ffentlichungen/Stellungnahmen_deutsch/einnahme-hoher-einzeldosen-vitamin-d-ueber-nahrungsergaenzungsmittel-im-abstand-von-tagen-oder-wochen-birgt-gesundheitliche-risiken.pdf)

#### Multivitamins
- A US long-term study (**>390,000** participants, up to **27 years**, about 165,000 deaths) found that daily multivitamins did not extend lifespan in healthy adults — [Thieme "Natürlich"](https://natuerlich.thieme.de/aktuelles/aus-der-forschung/detail/verlaengern-multivitamin-praeparate-das-leben-3562) (the primary study is not named in the snippet)
- Öko-Test, 17 "A–Z" multivitamins: 2 rated "mangelhaft" and 15 "ungenügend". They are usually superfluous and often overdosed — [Öko-Test](https://www.oekotest.de/gesundheit-medikamente/Vitamintabletten-Test-Was-Vitaminpraeparate-wirklich-bringen_10865_1.html); [t-online](https://www.t-online.de/leben/testberichte/id_86501972/-oeko-test-alle-17-vitaminpraeparate-fallen-durch-den-test.html); [apotheke adhoc](https://www.apotheke-adhoc.de/nachrichten/detail/panorama/multivitamine-ueberfluessig-und-ueberdosiert-oeko-test/) [date?; probably several years old]
- Supplements make sense only when a deficiency is medically proven or life circumstances make adequate supply hard — [foodwatch](https://www.foodwatch.org/de/wie-sinnvoll-sind-multivitamin-praeparate) (NGO) [the snippet did not say which result this came from]

#### Electrolytes and sodium for heavy sweaters
- Sweat sodium ranges from about 200 to 2,000 mg/L, averaging about 800–950 mg/L; "heavy or salty sweaters" lose about 1,500–2,500 mg/L — [Precision Hydration](https://www.precisionhydration.com/performance-advice/hydration/how-to-estimate-sweat-salt-loss/), [STYRKR](https://styrkr.com/en-us/blogs/training-and-nutrition-hub/how-much-sodium-do-you-lose-in-sweat) [weak; sellers of electrolyte products]
- ACSM-derived guidance, as reported by retailers:
  - 300–600 mg sodium per litre for 60–120-minute sessions, and 600–1,000 mg/L for >2 h or heat.
  - 300–700 mg/h for sessions over 60 min; 700–1,500 mg/h for heavy sweaters.
  - Sources: [calorique](https://calorique.io/blog/electrolytes-for-athletes/), [Saltivate](https://saltivate.com/pages/electrolytes-for/heavy-sweaters) [weak; second-hand ACSM attribution]
- In long, hot events with high fluid intake, drinking only water can lower plasma sodium (**hyponatraemia**), especially in salty sweaters — [Precision Hydration](https://www.precisionhydration.com/performance-advice/hydration/how-to-estimate-sweat-salt-loss/) [weak]

### Inferences
- **Who needs what: summary matrix for a German hypertrophy trainee** (derived from the findings above):

| Supplement | Default for a healthy omnivore | Who should consider it | Test first? | Safe ceiling to respect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vitamin D3 | 20 µg (800 IU)/day, roughly Oct–Mar, if outdoor exposure is low | Almost everyone in winter; indoor workers; dark skin; covered skin | Not for ≤800 IU. **Yes** before long-term >800 IU or bolus products | ≤20 µg per BfR; avoid ≥4000 IU long term and weekly/10–20-day bolus products without a doctor |
| Omega-3 EPA+DHA | Not needed with fish 1–2×/week | Non-fish eaters, vegans (algae oil) | No (Omega-3 index not researched) | About 250–500 mg/day matches the EFSA DRV. Avoid ≥1 g/day without a doctor, especially with CV risk (AF signal). DHA alone ≤1 g |

| B12 | Not needed for omnivores | **Vegans: mandatory**; vegetarians: test or consider | Vegans/vegetarians: B12 or holoTC | – (BfR value not retrieved) |

| K2 | Not needed | No established indication | – | ≤25 µg K2 (BfR); **contraindicated without a doctor with coumarins** |

| Electrolytes/sodium | Not needed for typical ≤90-min indoor gym sessions | Long, hot, very sweaty sessions (>60–120 min), visible salt crusts | – | People with high blood pressure should not add sodium without a doctor |

- Vitamin D dosing arithmetic: 1 µg = 40 IU, so 20 µg = 800 IU and the BfR concern threshold of 4000 IU = 100 µg. The RKI seasonal data (52% deficient in winter vs 8.3% in summer) suggest supplementing mainly in the dark season. 25(OH)D: 50 nmol/L ≈ 20 ng/mL (conversion factor about 2.5).
- The AF signal comes mainly from medicine-grade ethyl-ester doses of ≥1 g/day in CV-risk populations. A young healthy trainee at 250–500 mg/day is far below the doses that produced the ~12–50% increases, but "more is better" dosing of 2–4 g/day is not a harmless default.
- Hypertrophy-specific benefit claims (for example vitamin D or magnesium raising strength in people who are not deficient) were not researched and should not be assumed.

## 2. Blood tests before supplementing, and what they cost in Germany

### Takeaway
Test only when there is a reason:
- **Ferritin together with Hb, transferrin saturation and CRP** before any iron.
- **B12 or holoTC** for vegans and vegetarians.
- **25(OH)D** only before high or long-term doses above 800 IU, or with symptoms or risk factors (routine screening has unclear benefit).
- **TSH** before iodine supplements if there is any thyroid history. Stop biotin before thyroid tests.

Self-pay prices in 2025/26: ferritin about €17–20, B12 about €17–20, holoTC about €32–38, 25(OH)D about €27–33 (IGeL), and a vegan panel about €120. Statutory health insurance (GKV) pays when a deficiency is clinically suspected.

### Cited Findings
- IGeL-Monitor rates **screening for vitamin D deficiency as "unklar"** (unclear). No studies examined the benefit or harm of screening adults without signs of deficiency. Possible harms are false-positive or false-negative results and overdiagnosis. It costs **€27–33**. If a deficiency is suspected, the Krankenkasse pays; otherwise it is a self-paid IGeL service — [IGeL-Monitor](https://igel-monitor.de/igel-a-z/igel/show/frueherkennungsuntersuchung-auf-vitamin-d-mangel.html) (Medizinischer Dienst Bund); [IGeL-Monitor press release, 9 Nov 2022](https://www.igel-monitor.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/2022-11-09-nutzen-von-frueherkennungsuntersuchungen-auf-vitamin-d-mangel-unklar.html); [t-online](https://www.t-online.de/gesundheit/krankheiten-symptome/untersuchung-und-behandlung/id_100534162/vitamin-d-test-wann-die-krankenkasse-die-kosten-uebernimmt.html)
- The Endocrine Society (Jun 2024) advises against routine 25(OH)D screening and follow-up testing in healthy adults — [Medscape](https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/dont-screen-vitamin-d-new-endo-society-guideline-2024a1000aez)
- Self-pay lab prices:
  - Mein Direktlabor: B12 €16.76, ferritin €16.76, holoTC €32.18 — [Mein Direktlabor price list](https://www.meindirektlabor.de/ueber-uns/preisliste/) (commercial self-pay lab) [date?]

Further IGeL price lists exist: [MDI Limbach Berlin 2025](https://www.mdi-limbach-berlin.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Berlin/PDF/04_Service/Individuelle_Gesundheitsleistungen/Preisliste_Laboruntersuchungen_2025.pdf), [Labor Bremen](https://www.mlhb.de/media/pbwdwyak/igel-preisliste.pdf) [title only]

### Inferences
- A sensible, low-cost approach for a healthy omnivorous trainee:
  - No mandatory panel.
  - With fatigue, a restrictive diet or a menstruating athlete: ferritin + CRP (+Hb/TSAT), about €20–45 self-pay, or GKV-covered if a doctor suspects deficiency.
  - Vegans: B12 or holoTC + ferritin, about €35–60.
  - 25(OH)D (about €30): only before long-term doses >800 IU, or to confirm a suspected deficiency.
  - TSH: before iodine supplements if there is any thyroid history (price not retrieved).
- Test 25(OH)D in late winter to see the low point; summer values overstate status (inferred from the RKI seasonal data in section 1).
- Because ferritin rises with inflammation, don't test during or right after an infection or unusually hard training blocks. Include CRP.

- How long to pause biotin before a blood draw was not sourced.
- Creatine raises serum creatinine and can make kidney-function estimates (eGFR) look worse. This is background knowledge, **unverified** here; trainees should tell the doctor they take creatine.

## 3. German/EU regulation: what it means for label doses

### Takeaway
In Germany, food supplements are **foods** under the Nahrungsergänzungsmittelverordnung (NemV): there is **no approval**, only a **notification to the BVL** before first sale. The BVL does not check safety, efficacy or doses, and the manufacturer carries full responsibility. There are still **no binding EU or German maximum levels** for vitamins and minerals. BfR proposals (updated 2021, 2024 and 2025) are non-binding, so labels can legally carry 4000 IU vitamin D or 400 mg magnesium. EU harmonization has been "planned since 2021", with no official levels as of the latest sources. Health claims are limited to EU-authorized wordings. New ingredients or forms need novel-food authorization.

### Cited Findings
- Food supplements are foods intended to supplement the normal diet (§1(1) NemV). They must be **notified to the BVL before first placing on the market**, only via the online form. **No official approval exists**, and "the BVL does not assess, test or approve notified supplements". Business operators are responsible for legal compliance and safety — [BVL: Nahrungsergänzungsmittel](https://www.bvl.bund.de/DE/Arbeitsbereiche/01_Lebensmittel/04_AntragstellerUnternehmen/03_NEM/lm_nahrungsErgMittel_basepage.html) (federal authority); [BVL press release 2025 "Keine Zulassung von Nahrungsergänzungsmitteln notwendig"](https://www.bvl.bund.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/01_lebensmittel/2025/2025_3_PM_NEM.html) [title only]; [Verbraucherzentrale "Wie sind NEM geregelt?"](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/lebensmittel/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel/wie-sind-nahrungsergaenzungsmittel-geregelt-28253); [IHK Frankfurt](https://www.frankfurt-main.ihk.de/recht/uebersicht-alle-rechtsthemen/lebensmittel-und-bedarfsgegenstaenderecht/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel-5195260)
- Timeline of BfR maximum-amount proposals:
  - 2021 overall update (15 Mar 2021) — [BfR 2021 PDF](https://www.bfr.bund.de/cm/343/aktualisierte-hoechstmengenvorschlaege-fuer-vitamine-und-mineralstoffe-in-nahrungsergaenzungsmitteln-und-angereicherten-lebensmitteln.pdf)
  - **Stellungnahme 006/2024 (22 Feb 2024)**, revised proposals — [BfR 006/2024](https://www.bfr.bund.de/cm/343/aktualisierte-hoechstmengenvorschlaege-fuer-vitamine-und-mineralstoffe-in-nahrungsergaenzungsmitteln-und-angereicherten-lebensmitteln-2024.pdf); [OpenAgrar record](https://www.openagrar.de/receive/openagrar_mods_00093798)

  - **065/2025 (iodine, 19 Dec 2025)**
  - The proposals are meant as a **basis for discussing legally binding EU maximum levels**.
  - In 2024 the BfR adjusted B6, vitamin D, selenium and folate/folic acid — [Eurofins](https://www.eurofins.com/en-de/food-testing/news/eu-maximum-levels-for-vitamins-minerals-and-trace-elements/) (lab company news)

- EU harmonization:
  - Since 2021 the Commission has planned maximum levels for supplements and fortified foods, based on EFSA's re-evaluation of ULs. That re-evaluation **is not complete**, and the Commission has made **no official statement on concrete levels**.
  - Nutrients likely affected: selenium, manganese, folic acid, B6, vitamin A/beta-carotene, vitamin E, vitamin D and iron.

### Inferences
- Because BfR limits are non-binding, a label dose above them is **legal but not endorsed**. Examples: 4000 IU D3 versus the BfR 800 IU; 400 mg magnesium versus 250 mg; 25 mg zinc versus 6.5 mg. The consumer has to do the per-daily-dose comparison with the BfR table in section 1.
- "Notified to the BVL", "Made in Germany" or "GMP" is **not** a safety or quality check. Consumer tests (Stiftung Warentest 12/2025: 2 of 25 vitamin D products failed; Öko-Test: half of magnesium products overdosed; all 17 multivitamins failed) show that label compliance with BfR advice is poor.
- Authorized claims such as "magnesium contributes to normal muscle function" are generic regulatory wording, not evidence of extra muscle growth in people who are not deficient.
- When several products are stacked (a multivitamin plus single D, Mg or Zn products), the per-product BfR maxima can be exceeded in total. The BfR designs its maxima assuming several sources of intake.

### Gaps
- The exact NemV labelling duties were not verified this session (background knowledge: recommended daily dose, a warning not to exceed it, a note that supplements do not replace a varied diet, and "keep out of reach of small children"). NemV implements Directive 2002/46/EC, which includes a positive list of permitted vitamin and mineral forms (background, **unverified**).
- The "significant amount" threshold for claims (commonly 15% of the nutrient reference value per portion under Regulation 1169/2011) was not confirmed.
- EU Article-8 restrictions (Regulation 1925/2006; for example green-tea catechins, monacolins, hydroxyanthracenes) and the German "Stoffliste" were not researched.
- There is no authoritative source on whether binding EU maximum levels are imminent as of September 2026.

## 4. Quality and doping: contamination rates, Kölner Liste/Informed Sport, NADA guidance, how to judge quality

### Takeaway
Contamination is documented and persistent:
- The classic Cologne study found **14.8% of 634** non-hormonal supplements (**11.6%** of those bought in Germany) contained undeclared prohormones.
- A **2026 systematic review** estimates **9–15%** of commercial supplements contain undeclared prohibited or pharmacologically active substances, **up to 38%** in some samples. These are mostly stimulants and anabolic agents, especially sibutramine and anabolic steroids.
- About **6.4–8.8% of doping cases** trace back to supplements.

The Kölner Liste and Informed Sport reduce but do not eliminate the risk. The **NADA advises against supplements without medical indication**. For recreational trainees the main danger is to health (sibutramine, DMAA and steroids in pre-workouts, fat burners and slimming products). For tested athletes it also means sanctions.

### Cited Findings
- Cologne anti-doping lab study:
  - **634 supplements from 215 suppliers in 13 countries**, tested Oct 2000 – Nov 2001. **94 (14.8%)** contained undeclared prohormones: 23 nandrolone plus testosterone, 64 testosterone only, 7 nandrolone only.
  - By country of purchase: NL 25.8%, AT 22.7%, UK 18.9%, US 18.8%, **DE 11.6%**. 91% were bought in shops or online.
  - Contamination occurred mostly at firms that also processed prohormones, which suggests cross-contamination on shared production lines.
  - Sources: [DOSB, Schänzer interview](https://www.dosb.de/aktuelles/news/detail/schaenzer-internationale-studie-belegt-gefahr-durch-nahrungsergaenzungsmittel) (sports federation) [date?; **data 25 years old**]; [Austrian Parliament document](https://www.parlament.gv.at/dokument/XXI/A/661/fnameorig_600542.html)
- **Systematic review, *Frontiers in Sports and Active Living*, March 2026** (literature 2010–2025): an estimated **9–15%** of commercially available supplements contain undeclared prohibited or pharmacologically active substances. They are mainly stimulants and anabolic agents; the most frequent are **sibutramine and anabolic-androgenic steroids**. About **6.4–8.8%** of reported doping cases result from undeclared substances in supplements — [Frontiers 2026](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sports-and-active-living/articles/10.3389/fspor.2026.1740663/full) (peer-reviewed SR; the snippet summary may merge figures)
- Reported contamination rose from **10–25% (2015)** to **10–38%** (research from 2021). A 2021 Dutch study (Duiven et al.) found **38%** contained undeclared banned substances — [Ukraine National Anti-Doping Center news](https://nadc.gov.ua/en/news/dietary-supplements-remain-a-source-of-risk-for-unintentional-doping-new-study); [Duiven et al. 2021 (ResearchGate)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/350350084_Undeclared_Doping_Substances_are_Highly_Prevalent_in_Commercial_Sports_Nutrition_Supplements); related review [PMC9054437](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9054437/) [title only]
- **NADA and the Kölner Liste**:
  - The list is **not a recommendation** to take supplements. NADA generally advises against supplements without a medical indication and without consulting physicians or nutritionists (for example at Olympic training centres).
  - The risk of contamination or counterfeiting can only be **minimized, not excluded**, even for listed products; "100% safety cannot be guaranteed".
  - Listed products are tested **at least once a year** at the Zentrum für Präventive Dopingforschung of the Deutsche Sporthochschule Köln (German Sport University Cologne).
  - The Kölner Liste has been integrated into the **NADA app**, and NADA reported a restructured Kölner Liste.
  - Sources: [NADA "Kölner Liste – neuer Aufbau, mehr Sicherheit"](https://www.nada.de/en/service/news/news-detail/koelner-liste-neuer-aufbau-mehr-sicherheit-fuer-athletinnen-und-athleten) [date?]; [NADA app news](https://www.nada.de/en/service/news/news-detail/nada-app-jetzt-auch-mit-koelner-liste); [DOSB](https://www.dosb.de/aktuelles/news/detail/nada-app-jetzt-auch-mit-koelner-liste); [Gemeinsam gegen Doping (NADA prevention portal)](https://www.gemeinsam-gegen-doping.de/was-ist-doping/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel); [Kölner Liste background](https://www.koelnerliste.com/hintergrund/); [NADA "Vorsicht vor Nahrungsergänzungsmitteln"](https://www.nada.de/en/service/news/news-detail/vorsicht-vor-nahrungsergaenzungsmitteln) [title only]
- **Informed Sport (LGC)**:
  - It says it is the only global third-party programme that tests **every batch before market release**, using **ISO 17025-accredited** methods against a broad range of prohibited substances with defined reporting thresholds.
  - The Kölner Liste and Informed Sport are separate programmes; a product is not automatically on both.
  - Sources: [Informed Sport: About](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/about) (the certifier's own site); [Informed Sport vs Informed Choice](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/about/sport-vs-choice); [MNSTRY FAQ](https://mnstry.zendesk.com/hc/de/articles/43277556346769-K%C3%B6lner-Liste) [weak; brand FAQ]. A critical view that certification does not guarantee doping-free products: [Iontmax](https://www.iontmax.com/de/sport-und-wissenschaft/informed-sport-tests-von-nahrungserganzungsmitteln--warum-sie-keine-dopingfreien-produkte-garantieren/) [weak; commercial]
- Examples of German consumer warnings (dates of individual warnings not shown):
  - **Sibutramine** in "Sleekfit Kahve L-Carnitine & Kolajen".
  - The Landesuntersuchungsamt Rheinland-Pfalz (state food-testing authority) warned about "150G Slim", "A1 L-Carnitine Lepidium" and "Zotreem Dream", which contain sibutramine (one also contains phenolphthalein).
  - The **BfR classifies DMAA as particularly dangerous**; it is also labelled "Geranienextrakt" or "Methylhexanamin".
  - "Liver-Tabs" were recalled over Cassia occidentalis (aloe-emodin).
  - Sources: [Verbraucherzentrale "Verbraucherwarnungen zu NEM"](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/lebensmittel/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel/achtung-beim-kauf-verbraucherwarnungen-zu-nahrungsergaenzungsmitteln-21536); [VZ NRW "Gesundheitsgefahr durch illegale Stoffe"](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.nrw/wissen/lebensmittel/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel/gesundheitsgefahr-durch-illegale-stoffe-13394); [VZ "Öffentliche Warnungen vor gefährlichen Sportlerprodukten"](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/lebensmittel/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel/oeffentliche-warnungen-vor-gefaehrlichen-sportlerprodukten-7518) [title only]; [NADA "Food recall due to counterfeiting with sibutramine"](https://www.nada.de/en/service/news/news-detail/food-recall-due-to-counterfeiting-with-sibutramine) [title only]; [VZ "Ungewollt gedopt"](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/lebensmittel/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel/gewonnen-und-trotzdem-verloren-ungewollt-gedopt-13400) [title only]
- Quality tests show dose problems in mainstream products: Stiftung Warentest 12/2025 (vitamin D, strongly overdosed products failed), Öko-Test (magnesium, half overdosed, critical additives) and Öko-Test multivitamins (all 17 failed) — see section 1.

### Inferences
- **Buying checklist for a German trainee** (inferred from the evidence above):
  1. Prefer **single-ingredient** products (creatine monohydrate, whey, D3, fish or algae oil) with a clear per-serving dose **at or below BfR maxima**.
  2. If competing in any tested federation, use only **batch-tested** products (Kölner Liste or Informed Sport) and check that your **batch number** is listed. Even then some risk remains, and strict liability applies (WADA principle; not sourced here).
  3. **Avoid high-risk categories**: pre-workouts, fat burners, slimming coffees and teas, "test boosters", "legal steroids", SARMs and prohormones, especially from non-EU webshops. These are where stimulants, sibutramine and steroids turn up.
  4. **Avoid proprietary blends** ("Matrix", "Complex" without per-ingredient doses): you cannot check doses against BfR limits or detect overdosing.
  5. Check warnings before buying: lebensmittelwarnung.de (the federal/state food warning portal) and Verbraucherzentrale warnings.
  6. Buy from established EU retailers or pharmacies. Remember that BVL notification is not an approval.
  7. Treat an unusually strong "effect" (jitteriness, a very fast pump, rapid weight loss) as a red flag for undeclared drugs.
- For non-competing gym-goers, a contaminated product mainly means health risk (cardiovascular events from stimulants, suppression of the body's own hormones from steroids) and possibly legal risk. For tested athletes, it can also end a career.

### Gaps
- How the German Anti-Doping Act (AntiDopG) applies to recreational athletes (for example possession of certain doping agents above threshold amounts) was not researched.
- The composition of the Duiven 2021 sample (reportedly higher-risk products bought online) was not verified, so the 38% may not represent all supplements.
- Kölner Liste testing scope and detection limits were not retrieved (site blocked). NSF Certified for Sport was not researched.
- There are no recent (2020s) Germany-specific contamination figures; the 11.6% German figure dates from 2000–2001.

## 5. Interactions and contraindications with common medications; who should see a doctor first

### Takeaway
The most relevant risks:
- **Vitamin K/K2 weakens coumarin anticoagulants.**
- **Omega-3 at ≥1 g/day** raises AF risk in people with heart disease or CV risk.
- **Calcium and iron** (magnesium disputed) block **levothyroxine** absorption; keep them 2–4 h apart.
- **Biotin** falsifies thyroid lab values.
- **Iodine** is not for people with thyroid disease without a doctor.
- **Potassium** products plus ACE inhibitors, ARBs or potassium-sparing diuretics can cause hyperkalaemia, especially with kidney disease, older age or diabetes.
- **St John's wort** can cause serotonin syndrome with antidepressants and weakens many drugs through enzyme induction.

Anyone on anticoagulants, antidepressants, thyroid or blood-pressure medication, or with heart, kidney or thyroid disease or pregnancy, should see a doctor or pharmacist first.

### Cited Findings
- **Anticoagulants:** vitamin K1 and K2 can weaken coumarin-type anticoagulants, so a doctor must be consulted first — [BfR vitamin K](https://www.bfr.bund.de/cm/343/hoechstmengenvorschlaege-fuer-vitamin-k-in-lebensmitteln-inklusive-nahrungsergaenzungsmitteln.pdf); [VZ Vitamin K](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/lebensmittel/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel/vitaminkprodukte-was-ist-sinnvoll-26574). St John's wort also affects "some blood thinners" — [MSD Manual (German patient edition)](https://www.msdmanuals.com/de/heim/spezialthemen/nahrungserg%C3%A4nzungsmittel-und-vitamine/johanniskraut)
- **Heart and rhythm:** dose-dependent AF risk with omega-3 in CV patients or CV-risk groups; seek care for palpitations — [EMA DHPC 2023](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/dhpc/direct-healthcare-professional-communication-dhpc-omega-3-acid-ethyl-ester-medicines-dose-dependent-increased-risk-atrial-fibrillation-patients-established-cardiovascular-diseases-or-cardiovascular_en.pdf); [BfR](https://www.bfr.bund.de/veroeffentlichung/praeparate-mit-omega-3-fettsaeuren-koennen-bei-herzpatienten-das-risiko-fuer-vorhofflimmern-erhoehen/). Vitamin D overdose can cause arrhythmias through hypercalcaemia — [BfR 065/2023](https://www.bfr.bund.de/cm/343/hochdosierte-nahrungsergaenzungsmittel-mit-vitamin-d-koennen-langfristig-die-gesundheit-beeintraechtigen.pdf)
- **Thyroid medication:**
  - Levothyroxine interacts with the polyvalent cations **calcium, iron and aluminium**. **For magnesium this interaction seems not to occur** (PZ 2007).
  - Keep **≥2 h, better 4 h**, between levothyroxine and minerals. Take levothyroxine in the morning on an empty stomach with water, at least 30 min before breakfast.
  - Sources: [Pharmazeutische Zeitung 45/2007](https://www.pharmazeutische-zeitung.de/ausgabe-452007/schilddruesenhormone-und-polyvalente-kationen/) (pharmacists' journal; **old**); [Forum Schilddrüse 2023](https://www.forum-schilddruese.de/service/schilddruese-news/schilddruese-news-2023/13-l-thyroxin-wechselwirkung-arzneimittel-medikamente-nahrungsergaenzung-lebensmittel); [medonline](https://medonline.at/ch/news/medizin/10045449/l-thyroxin-nicht-zusammen-mit-mineralien-oder-eisen-schlucken/); [apotheke adhoc, L-thyroxine and calcium](https://www.apotheke-adhoc.de/nachrichten/detail/pta-live/repetitorium-schilddruesenhormone-l-thyroxin-interaktionsgefahr-calcium/)
  - [conflict] Other pharmacy sources list magnesium among interacting minerals — [Apotheke Große](https://apotheke-grosse.de/haeufige-wechselwirkungen-l-thyroxin-und-mineralstoffe/) [title only]
- **Thyroid labs:** biotin interferes with the measurement of thyroid values — [Forum Schilddrüse 2023](https://www.forum-schilddruese.de/service/schilddruese-news/schilddruese-news-2023/13-l-thyroxin-wechselwirkung-arzneimittel-medikamente-nahrungsergaenzung-lebensmittel)
- **Thyroid disease:** people with thyroid disease should avoid iodine supplements — [BfR 065/2025](https://www.bfr.bund.de/assets/01_Ver%C3%B6ffentlichungen/Stellungnahmen_deutsch/aktualisierung-2025-h%C3%B6chstmengenvorschl%C3%A4ge-f%C3%BCr-jod-in-lebensmitteln-inklusive-nahrungserg%C3%A4nzungsmitteln.pdf). Algae products and Lugol's solution carry iodine-overdose warnings — [VZ](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/lebensmittel/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel/oft-zu-viel-jod-in-meeresalgenprodukten-8540), [BfR 027/2026](https://www.bfr.bund.de/assets/01_Ver%C3%B6ffentlichungen/Mitteilungen_deutsch/lugolsche-loesung-ist-keine-loesung-zur-verbesserung-der-jodversorgung.pdf) [title only]
- **Blood-pressure medication and potassium:**
  - ACE inhibitors, ARBs (sartans), beta-blockers and potassium-sparing diuretics raise potassium, and their effects add up.
  - Hyperkalaemia usually needs extra risk factors: kidney impairment, older age, diabetes, or spironolactone >25 mg/day.
  - **Potassium supplements are not for self-medication** in patients on potassium-raising drugs.
  - Symptoms: muscle weakness and paraesthesias (numb "furry" tongue, tingling).
  - Sources: [PZ 15/2008](https://www.pharmazeutische-zeitung.de/ausgabe-152008/kaliumsparende-diuretika-und-ace-hemmer/); [apotheke adhoc](https://www.apotheke-adhoc.de/nachrichten/detail/pta-live/blutdrucksenker-kaliumsparende-diuretika-hyperkaliaemie/); [PZ 42/2014](https://www.pharmazeutische-zeitung.de/ausgabe-422014/kaliumhaushalt-im-blick/); [arznei-telegramm 1998](https://www.arznei-telegramm.de/html/1998_02/9802027_01.html) (independent drug bulletin; **old**)
- **Antidepressants and St John's wort:** St John's wort activates enzymes and transport proteins, so many drugs are broken down faster and work less well: immunosuppressants, HIV drugs, certain cancer drugs, some anticoagulants, **hormonal contraceptives**. **With antidepressants there is a risk of serotonin syndrome** — [MSD Manual](https://www.msdmanuals.com/de/heim/spezialthemen/nahrungserg%C3%A4nzungsmittel-und-vitamine/johanniskraut); [MSD interaction table](https://www.msdmanuals.com/de/heim/multimedia/table/einige-wechselwirkungen-mit-medikamenten-bei-johanniskraut); [DAK](https://www.dak.de/dak/gesundheit/koerper-seele/wechselwirkungen-von-medikamenten_135520) (statutory insurer)
- **Bolus vitamin D plus K:** only under medical supervision — [BfR 31/2025](https://www.bfr.bund.de/assets/01_Ver%C3%B6ffentlichungen/Stellungnahmen_deutsch/einnahme-hoher-einzeldosen-vitamin-d-ueber-nahrungsergaenzungsmittel-im-abstand-von-tagen-oder-wochen-birgt-gesundheitliche-risiken.pdf)
- **Zinc and copper:** chronic high zinc intake leads to copper-metabolism disturbance and anaemia — [VZ](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/lebensmittel/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel/mehr-zink-bei-erkaeltungen-13390)

### Inferences
- **See a doctor or pharmacist before starting if any of these apply:**
  - Anticoagulants such as coumarins (for example phenprocoumon or warfarin): no vitamin K/K2 without a doctor; ask about fish oil ≥1 g and St John's wort.
  - Heart disease, arrhythmia or several CV risk factors: omega-3 ≥1 g/day, stimulant pre-workouts.
  - Thyroid disease or levothyroxine: iodine, biotin before labs, spacing of calcium, iron and magnesium.
  - Blood-pressure medication (ACE inhibitors, ARBs, potassium-sparing diuretics): potassium or "electrolyte" products high in potassium.
  - Antidepressants: St John's wort and other serotonergic supplements.
  - Kidney disease: magnesium, potassium, high-protein or creatine plans.
  - Pregnancy or breastfeeding: separate BfR limits apply, for example iodine 150 µg.
  - Any plan for vitamin D >800 IU/day, bolus products or iron.
- Practical rule for levothyroxine users: take levothyroxine alone in the morning, and minerals (Ca, Fe; Mg to be safe given the conflict) ≥4 h later.

### Gaps
- Omega-3 bleeding risk in people already on anticoagulants or antiplatelet drugs was not sourced (EFSA 2012 found no bleeding increase up to 5 g/day in the general population only).
- 5-HTP or tryptophan with SSRIs (serotonin syndrome), caffeine or stimulants with antihypertensives or MAO inhibitors, liquorice with blood pressure, and magnesium, zinc, calcium or iron with tetracycline or quinolone antibiotics and bisphosphonates were **not sourced** this session. They are well-known pharmacology but unverified here.
- Creatine with kidney disease or nephrotoxic drugs (for example regular NSAIDs), and creatinine confounding eGFR, were not sourced.
- How long to pause biotin before a blood draw was not sourced.

## 6. Typical monthly costs in Germany (EUR) for a sensible base stack

### Takeaway
A sensible base stack costs roughly **€35–55 per month** including protein powder. Protein is by far the largest item: about **€25–40/month** for 30 g/day, and whey concentrate prices rose about **42% in 10 months**, to a median of about **€34/kg in May 2026**. The rest: creatine about €3–10, vitamin D about €1–3 (winter months only), omega-3 about €2–27 (typically €5–15, only for non-fish eaters) and magnesium a few euros (not retrieved). Without protein powder the micronutrient and creatine part is about **€10–30/month**. One-off self-pay lab checks cost about €20–120.
